Further analysis coming soon.

NRIM (New Right Infrastructure Map) is an exploratory visual project mapping how cultural actors, platforms, organisations, and media ecosystems convert attention into durable political capacity.

Rather than focusing on ideology alone, the project traces functional relationships: amplification, monetisation, recruitment, and institutionalisation.

Nodes represent actors or institutions; links represent operational relationships rather than formal affiliation.

The map is designed as a research and sense-making tool, not a comprehensive or normative taxonomy.
